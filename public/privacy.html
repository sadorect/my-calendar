Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Birth Calendar is provided by Sadorect ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: what you write stays on your device unless you turn on sync, and if you do, it is encrypted before it leaves your device so we cannot read it.

What stays on your device

Your due date, children's profiles, favourites, notes, prayers, progress and settings are stored in your browser's own storage on the device you are using. They are not sent to us. Clearing the app's site data, or using Reset in Settings, deletes them.

Daily reminders are scheduled by your device and shown as local notifications. The biometric lock uses your device's own fingerprint or face check; we never receive biometric data. Shared declaration images are drawn on your device and passed to whatever app you choose to share them with.

Optional sync across devices

If you create a sync account, our server (birthapp.sadorect.com) stores:

  • your email address, used as your account name;
  • a derived login secret, hashed again on the server — never your password;
  • an encrypted copy of your app data, locked with a key that is derived from your password on your device and never sent to us;
  • when the account was created and last used, and the size and version of that copy.

Because only you hold the key, we cannot read your data, and we cannot recover it if you forget your password. We do not use your email address for marketing and we do not send you email.

You can delete the server copy at any time from Settings → Sync across devices → Delete from server. That removes your account and the encrypted data from our database. You can also ask us to do it at the address below.

Usage statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies and does not build a profile of you; it records anonymous information such as the page visited, referring site, country, browser and device type. We also count the number of sync accounts and how many have synced recently. Neither tells us anything about what you have written.

Hosting and logs

The app is served by Vercel, and the sync service runs on our own server behind Cloudflare. Like any web service, these providers process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and protect against abuse, and may keep short-lived logs.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • No selling or renting of your information.
  • No reading of your notes, prayers or children's details — we can't.

Children's information

The app lets parents and carers keep names and dates for their children. That information stays on your device, or is stored encrypted if you use sync. The app is intended for adults; children should not create sync accounts.

Your rights

You can see, change and delete everything you keep in the app yourself. For the sync account, you can delete it from the app, or contact us to ask what we hold, to correct it or to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will say so in the app.
